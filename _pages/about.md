I am an undergraduate in Electrical &amp; Computer Engineering at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My work sits at the intersection of **computational physics** and **deep generative modeling**: I am interested in how physical structure — flows, trajectories, contacts, simulated geometry — can be turned into signals that generative video models actually use, so that the videos they produce are physically consistent and can be checked rather than merely plausible.

I came to this from the physics side. A computational-physics competition and 3D aerodynamic simulation taught me to translate physical assumptions into models whose outputs can be quantitatively verified; I now bring that same "model it, then verify it" habit to generative video and world models.

## Research Interests

<div class="ac-grid">
  <div class="ac-card">
    <div class="ac-card__icon"><i class="fa-solid fa-wind" aria-hidden="true"></i></div>
    <p class="ac-card__title">Physics-Enhanced Video Generation</p>
    <p class="ac-card__body">Conditioning video models on optical flow, trajectories, simulated point clouds and reference video so that motion, contact and occlusion stay physically consistent over time.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ac-card">
    <div class="ac-card__icon"><i class="fa-solid fa-cubes" aria-hidden="true"></i></div>
    <p class="ac-card__title">Multimodal World Models</p>
    <p class="ac-card__body">Interactive, controllable world models that combine visual, geometric and language conditioning to predict how a scene evolves under actions.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ac-card">
    <div class="ac-card__icon"><i class="fa-solid fa-diagram-project" aria-hidden="true"></i></div>
    <p class="ac-card__title">Generative Modeling &amp; Distillation</p>
    <p class="ac-card__body">Diffusion / flow models and few-step distillation — in particular the error accumulation that appears when few-step samplers are reused across many steps.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="ac-card">
    <div class="ac-card__icon"><i class="fa-solid fa-circle-check" aria-hidden="true"></i></div>
    <p class="ac-card__title">Trustworthy Evaluation</p>
    <p class="ac-card__body">Protocols that test whether a model truly grounds its outputs in the given conditions, using zero / random / mismatched / real-condition controls instead of surface metrics alone.</p>
  </div>
</div>
